# Territories are always the hardest Level, regardless of population

Difficulty is set by Population Rank, but only among Countries (the 193 UN members plus Vatican City, Palestine, Taiwan and Kosovo). Every Territory goes into Level 5 (Super Difficult), even where its population is larger than many Countries' — Hong Kong and Puerto Rico would otherwise land in Levels 2–3. Territory flags are far less familiar than population suggests, many resemble each other (e.g. British overseas territories), and the player chose to treat everything beyond the Countries as a separate, harder category.

## Consequences

Vatican City, Palestine, Taiwan and Kosovo are deliberately counted as Countries, not Territories, so they are placed by population like the 193.
